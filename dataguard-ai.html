DataGuard AI

Catches bad data before it reaches a decision, and says what broke and since when.

  • 22 of 24 root causes ranked first (taxi)
  • 22.4M rows in 84 s
  • 12 fault types
View on GitHub

Problem

Pipelines pass bad data through silently. Nothing errors, a dashboard simply becomes wrong, and the question that matters later is not just what broke but since when. DataGuard AI is built to answer both.

Data

NYC taxi
12 injected fault types across 24 detected experiments.
Vehicle Energy Dataset
384 cars, 54 weekly files, 22.4M rows (3.2 GB), rebuilt in 84 s.
Registration
Raw files registered with SHA-256 hashes so an unchanged file is never reprocessed.
Rules
Row rules declared in YAML; dataset checks for schema, volume, freshness, NULL rate and drift.

Approach

  • Drift measured with PSI against up to six months of history, producing a quality score and a health label.
  • Isolation Forest catches trips that are plausible on every individual rule but inconsistent taken together.
  • Incidents open only when a rule's failure rate rises above its own history, and resolve after two clean months.
  • Where/when analysis pins the breakage to a partition and a date range.
  • The explainer works without a language model, and says “cause unknown” when confidence is low.
  • Suggested actions always wait for human approval — nothing is auto-applied.

Real finding

From April 2018, up to 52 petrol cars stopped sending fuel trims. That was not an injected fault — the pipeline found it in the real data.

Engineering story

DuckDB’s CSV reader silently skipped the first 32 MB of each file, losing 53.5% of rows without raising anything. Fixed with a pyarrow preparation step — which is exactly the class of failure this project exists to catch.

Limitations
& honest notes

  • Phase 10 of 11. Still in development.
  • The full-taxi-data evaluation is still pending — the 22 of 24 result is on the detected experiments, not the complete dataset.
  • On the Vehicle Energy Dataset, 18 of 24 fault experiments were caught with a clean control month; the right cause was ranked first in all 18 of those, but six experiments were not detected at all.
