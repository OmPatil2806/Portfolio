AI Fitness Coach

Tracks workouts and calories, and forecasts your weight 30 days ahead.

  • ~0.29 kg weight forecast MAE
  • ~32 kcal calorie-burn MAE
  • 8 pages
View on GitHub Live demo

Problem

Tracking apps tell you what you already did. This one turns the log into a forecast: where your weight is heading over the next 30 days, what five different plans would do to it, and what to change — without needing an API key or sending data anywhere.

Data

Storage
SQLite tables: user_profile, weight_log, workout_log, calorie_log.
Training data
Models ship pre-trained on synthetic data and can be retrained on the user's own logs inside the app.
Chatbot
Rule-based across 15+ topics, using the user's profile. Not an LLM.

Approach

  • Dashboard — KPIs, monthly heatmap, progress ring, insight cards.
  • Health Metrics — Fitness Score 0–100, Health Age, BMI, waist-to-height ratio, body fat %, water targets.
  • Workout Tracker — sets, reps, intensity, MET-based calorie burn, personal records, streaks, weekly planner.
  • Calorie Tracker — quick-add for 10 foods, meal timing, calorie-to-goal ETA.
  • Body Intelligence — weight trend, 30/60/90-day projections, consistency grades A–D.
  • Predictions & AI — 30-day weight forecast, what-if comparison of five plans, calorie burn estimator, exercise ranking.
  • AI Coach — rule-based chatbot over 15+ topics, drawing on the user's profile.

Honest note

Both models are pre-trained on synthetic data, so these errors describe the generator, not real people. The app can retrain them on your own logs. The coach is a rule-based chatbot, not a language model.

Roadmap: Claude-based chatbot, PDF progress reports, Open Food Facts search, PostgreSQL storage, Google Fit and Apple Health sync.

Limitations
& honest notes

  • Models are pre-trained on synthetic data — real-world error would differ.
  • The coach is rule-based across a fixed set of topics, not a language model.
  • Not medical or nutritional advice.
